Executive Summary InstaVault is a native Android application that lets users download any Instagram media — posts, reels, stories, highlights, carousels, and profile pictures — by simply pasting a link or sharing directly from the Instagram app. It is inspired by the open-source philosophy of Seal (a yt-dlp-based Android video downloader) and brings the same reliability, quality selection, and clean UX to Instagram-specific media. 2. Problem Statement Instagram does not provide a native "save to device" option for posts, reels, or stories. Existing third-party solutions are: Pain Point Current Reality Web-only Cannot download from within a native app workflow Intrusive ads Banner/interstitial ads on every action Privacy-invasive Require login, scrape user credentials No quality selection Force one resolution regardless of source Unreliable Break whenever Instagram updates its front-end No batch download Cannot grab all media from a carousel at once No history Downloads disappear after session ends InstaVault solves all of these by running yt-dlp on a private backend, presenting a clean native Android UI, and never requiring Instagram credentials. 3. Target Users Persona Use Case Content Creator Save competitor/inspiration reels for offline study Social Media Manager Archive client content and brand assets Casual User Save a friend's funny reel or story before it expires Researcher/Journalist Archive public posts for documentation 4.
